Finding this picture among my photos from years ago put a huge smile on my face.

This is a discarded part of the Large Hadron Collider.

I doubt, criticise and am generally pretty hard on myself like most people. Last year I identified this as a major obstacle to my unfolding potential and practiced loving and accepting myself.

When I took this photo I thought it's a bit over the top, "What will people think? What a show off."

Now, when I look at it I think: "Wow, I love this crazy guy."
