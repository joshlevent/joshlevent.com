Unsigned is a building you walk through in your browser.

We spend our days in environments that are full of signs. What would it be like to enter a building that sheds it's signs and symbols gradually? A kind of retreat to nature, but going in, rather than going out.

## 





## Making of

This project was vibe-coded with Claude. I provided a vision, and many rounds of feedback and revision to come closer to that vision, but gave essentially no technical input. The only technical decision I did make is that the whole thing should run entirely in the browser.
