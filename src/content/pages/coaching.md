I became a coach almost by accident. In 2012 I was leading a large non-profit in New Zealand focused on youth leadership development, when a former colleague asked me to coach her. A few months later we were both changed: she landed her dream job, and I decided to become a coach.

I trained in the Art and Science of Coaching at Erickson College, founded by Marilyn Atkinson on the work of Milton Erickson, and coached as a member of the International Coach Federation. The science of coaching comes from humanistic and positive psychology; the art from listening, understanding and shaping each other. I held myself to a high professional standard, and I also knew its limits: coaching is emotionally demanding work I enjoyed but could not do forty hours a week, which is why it stayed a secondary profession.

## Who I worked with

**Founders.** Solutions-focused coaching, facilitation of management-team discussions, and leadership training for technology start-ups as they scaled. This drew on seven years in leadership development with AIESEC across six countries, and on my own time founding, advising and working for start-ups in New Zealand, Switzerland, Germany, Italy and the USA.

**Speakers.** Preparation for TED and TEDx talks, from the story to the delivery. Having given [two TEDx talks](/talks) myself, I helped clients prepare talks worth remembering. My method is in the guide [How to prepare for a TED Talk](/how-to-prepare-for-a-ted-talk), which is still the most useful thing on this page.

**Therapists and healing professionals.** Hypnotherapists, kinesiologists, bodywork and breathwork practitioners, yoga teachers: people with a real expertise and no marketing background, whom I helped find more clients in a sustainable and authentic way.

**Individuals.** People with big goals who wanted a partner, not a guru, to become more successful, peaceful or fulfilled. My coaching values were adventure, wisdom and courage: we grow through challenge, we achieve wisdom by integrating our emotional, creative, logical and practical intelligence, and what we fear is what we must do.

I coached in English and German, in person in Bern and remotely, from 2012 until I wound down the practice in 2020. I am no longer taking on clients; what I learned about change and attention now goes into my [teaching](/teaching).
