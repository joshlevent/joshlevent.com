*Updated October 2026. (Now page inspired by [Derek Sivers](https://nownownow.com/about).)*

In January I finished my second Master's degree, an MSc in Applied Economics at the University of Neuchâtel, with a [thesis](/economics) on whether agent-based model forecasts add incremental information in macroeconomic forecasting. Since then most of my economics time has gone into the [Macroeconomic Outlook](https://macroeconomic-outlook.joshlevent.com/), a site that collects official data and publications for 65 economies and updates itself daily.

I still teach at the Bern University of Applied Sciences. The [Power BI Bootcamp](/power-bi) runs several times a year and I continue to teach the [Present your Projects](/teaching) workshop in the Master of Design once a year.

I'm currently looking for a 60% job where I can apply my skills alongside my teaching and parenting. A few side projects have been brewing:  a [location dossier](/projects/standortdossier) for Swiss home buyers that builds a report on any address from public registers and geodata, and [Unsigned](/art/unsigned), a walk through a building whose signs thin out room by room until there are none.

I stepped down from the volunteer fire service in 2025 after five years. I read sci-fi, I am still fascinated by David Deutsch's theory of knowledge, and I spend a good deal of time thinking about what AI does to work, including my own.
