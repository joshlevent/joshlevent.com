# Humans with Josh Levent

A podcast for people who want the world to slow down and become more human. Josh Levent creates a space for empathetic connections to people on the other side of the planet through biographical interviews of ordinary people.

**Listen in your podcast app:** [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525) · [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/5Z41wAgICMgKXZpVAxUQzw)

## Episodes

**Episode 10 — Playful Curiosity, Exploring the World Headfirst: Meet Christofer Lövgren.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7fPylxNvRN2eRwWhBfaYjQ) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000538362401)) Christofer is the host of the *Do Explain* podcast, exploring David Deutsch and Critical Rationalism. We talk guitar, concussions, curiosity, burnout and the tragedy of thinking learning and fun are opposites.

**Episode 9 — Becoming Yourself Again: Meet Kai Christen.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/7mAKOgOJNQi38FrVgRDUI8) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000535051180)) From a shy, sensitive child to a confident trainer running personal-leadership modules at Swiss universities. Personal development, radical honesty and minimalism.

**Episode 8 — The Big Picture, Connecting the Dots of Experience: Meet Tinashe Mashungu.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5htKpN7zv00cqztDssEGwL) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000435026469)) Born in Harare, Tinashe studied physics, maths and economics and wants to democratise energy provision across Africa.

**Episode 7 — Finding Balance in the Passionate Pursuit of Life: Meet Laura Curau.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/13oWYsa2BsYm0hIouJwaLR) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000430400545)) From a small Swiss town to national campaign manager for one of the largest political parties in Switzerland — work-life balance, asking for help, and knowing when to quit.

**Episode 6 — Relearning How to Walk in Confidence: Meet Bongiwe Beja.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/4SQrMUSMk9Hd6ntNOyG1wA) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000429847172)) Born in Mthatha, head percussionist of a youth orchestra by 15, Bongiwe rewrote her story through the grief of divorce and learned to walk in confidence again.

**Episode 5 — Constantly Seeking the Truth: Meet Yushan Chen.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3CPPueHiXQbmA83RI2pJk7) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000427856086)) A Chinese filmmaker now in Los Angeles on a strict upbringing, discovering rock music, overcoming depression, and filmmaking.

**Episode 4 — Searching for His Role in Life: Meet Varun Venugopal Gupta.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2rbEGCfDhMDpS0CUEk4qzJ) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000427382464)) Raised independent in India, a love of theatre, and regaining confidence after a difficult school experience.

**Episode 3 — On a Journey to Find Meaningful Work: Meet Gemma Milne.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/57P29GpwOgrfjUTYRGcRPW) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000426334119)) A British science writer on the search for meaningful work, the desire for attention, Space School and a transition to science writing.

**Episode 2 — Empathy Is the Answer: Meet Business Analyst Mosidi Modise.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/61LAYGchzoluoHPm4rfKkC) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000425821163)) A South African social entrepreneur and finance intrapreneur at Allan Gray raising awareness around sexual harassment and reducing homelessness.

**Episode 1 — Overcoming Failure: Meet Filmmaker & Entrepreneur Francis Rafal.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2QpDp737o3tV1JLXIqOrDr) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000425274791)) An Austrian filmmaker and entrepreneur on playfulness, near-bankruptcy, and building back with the support of his network.

**Trailer.** ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6A5ca69ms4lMIqEr7qoUfO) · [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/ch/podcast/humans-with-josh-levent/id1441960525?i=1000423677951)) Take a moment to catch your breath, hit pause on life and curl up on a sofa with *Humans* in your earbuds.
