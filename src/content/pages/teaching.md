## Earlier teaching

- **viscom, Design Sprint (2019 and 2020).** A course on design methodologies and no-code prototyping, part of the vocational training for Interactive Media Designers.
- **Conference facilitation (2007 onwards).** Hundreds of sessions, trainings and strategic meetings, first with AIESEC across six countries, then at public conferences and for companies in Switzerland.
- **Coaching students.** As part of my teaching I coach students solving real-world problems set by case partners. See also [Coaching](/coaching).
