I believe that we can unlock the potential of billions more people by helping each other advance our knowledge, supercharging our altruistic instincts, and dampening our selfish ones.

I am a high-level [designer](/writing/what-is-business-design), working on concepts, organisations and other systems, to deliver real human value in a sustainable way.

I am an [economist](/research) studying the relationships between human behaviour and economic outcomes with the goal of developing better systems to make humanity more successful.

I am an external lecturer and expert at the Bern University of Applied Science, and occasionally lecture at other Swiss universities and vocational schools on a visiting basis.

As part of my [teaching](/teaching), I coach students in solving real world problems they are presented by case partners.

From 2020 to 2025 I was a volunteer firefighter in my local fire service, and from 2022 to 2026 I completed a second Master Degree in Applied Economics as part of my ongoing [research interests](/research).

From 2018 until 2020 I ran my own company, Josh Levent LLC, where I produced a [podcast](/podcast), consulted clients, and coached small business owners.

I was the Curator 18-19 of the [Global Shapers Hub Bern](https://www.globalshapers.org/community-details/a0eb0000000OH0GAAW), a volunteer organisation where we seek to make a local impact for a better world using cross-sector collaboration.

From June 2016 until September 2018 I was designing solutions for clients as Chief Innovation Officer of [Muutu](http://muutu.ch), a technology design studio in Bern, Switzerland. We were also internally developing a virtual retirement home solution to solve the biggest challenges in elderly care today.

I completed my Master in Business Design at Domus Academy, in Milan Italy, a global top 30 Design School.

I also graduated from the world-class Draper University immersive entrepreneurship programme, and completed the Art and Science of Coaching at Erickson College.

I was Head of Marketing for a medium-sized technology services company in Bern, Switzerland from 2012 to 2014.

Prior to that I was the President (CEO) of a large non-profit organisation called AIESEC New Zealand.

I completed my undergraduate studies in International Business and Management at the University of Auckland, New Zealand.

## Impact

Making an impact is core to my worldview and I do this both through my work, but also by supporting organisations I believe in. Currently, the one I believe in the most is [GiveDirectly](https://www.givedirectly.org).

## Angel Investments

As an angel investor I put my money in a small number of private startups whose mission I believe in. As of August 2026 I have stakes in [Neurohacker Collective](https://www.neurohacker.com/) and [Atom Limbs](https://atomlimbs.com/).
